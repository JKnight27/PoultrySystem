# JamesKnight Poultry System — Chinese Silkie Inventory

## Product 1: 4 Months Old Chinese Silkie (Grower Stage)

- **Visual Profile**: Silver/Blue plumage with light grey hues, prominent bright turquoise earlobes, dark comb/wattles, heavily feathered shanks and feet.
- **Growth Stage**: 16 Weeks (Pullet / Cockerel)
- **Characteristics**: Fast-growing, high vitality, developing crest, lightweight frame.
- **Stock Status**: Active / In Stock
- **Target Use**: Flock integration, juvenile raising, custom breeding projects.

### Key Characteristics at 4 Months Old
- **Feathers**: Fluffy, fur-like down feathers rather than hard, smooth feathers.
- **Skin & Bones**: Naturally dark blue/black skin and bones (a hallmark of the breed).
- **Toes**: Distinctive 5-toed feet instead of the standard 4.
- **Maturity**: At 16 weeks, pullets are getting close to point-of-lay (usually starting around 20–24 weeks).

## Product 2: 5 Months Old Chinese Silkie (Point-of-Lay)

- **Visual Profile**: Dense Charcoal/Grey fluffy down, full rounded crest, compact body silhouette, walnut-style comb emerging.
- **Growth Stage**: 20 Weeks (Sub-Adult)
- **Characteristics**: Nearing egg production, calm social behavior, highly cold-hardy down feathers.
- **Stock Status**: High Demand / Limited Stock
- **Target Use**: Egg production starter, backyard pet, premium breeding stock.

### Key Characteristics at 5 Months Old
- **Point of Lay (Females)**: Pullets typically lay their very first eggs around 20 to 24 weeks old. Their abdomen widens and their comb becomes slightly larger.
- **Crows & Crests (Males)**: Cockrels are usually crowing consistently by 5 months. Their crest feathers sweep back into a "streamer" shape, unlike the rounded pom-pom crest of females.
- **Full Plumage Density**: The fur-like down feathers reach full volume and thickness, hiding their dark skin almost completely except around the face and wattles.

## Product 3: 6 Months Old Chinese Silkie (Mature Breeder)

- **Visual Profile**: Pure Silk-White pom-pom plumage, fully developed crest and beard cushion, black skin/legs, solid adult stance.
- **Growth Stage**: 24 Weeks (Fully Mature)
- **Characteristics**: Ready for immediate laying/breeding, broodiness traits unlocked, showcase quality.
- **Stock Status**: Ready for Dispatch
- **Target Use**: Direct egg harvesting, breeding programs, show/exhibition.

### Key Characteristics at 6 Months Old
- **Active Egg Laying**: Hens are officially laying eggs regularly (typically 3–4 small cream-colored eggs per week).
- **Definitive Crest & Head Features**:
  - Hens display a dense, perfectly round "pom-pom" crest that sometimes partially obstructs their vision.
  - Roosters develop longer, swept-back crest feathers along with a walnut-shaped comb that turns a deep mulberry or dark purple color.
- **Full Size & Weight**: They have reached their maximum adult weight—usually 2 to 3 lbs for bantams (the standard in North America) or up to 3.5 to 4.5 lbs for large fowl varieties.
- **Broodiness Begins**: Around this age, Silkie hens frequently display their famous "broody" instinct, sitting persistently on eggs to hatch them.
